The ones you can't make yourself

These must be HPD's or FDNY's approved form, not a lookalike. Owners have been fined for posting signs that read correctly but don't match the official specification — especially the combined smoke/CO/gas notice. Download the real ones.

Get these from the agency

  • Smoke and CO detecting devices notice — 28 RCNY § 12-01, HMC § 27-2046.1 May be posted as a combined notice with gas leak procedures. Must be the HPD-approved form.
  • Gas leak procedures notice — Local Law 153 of 2016 Must follow HPD's graphical specification exactly.
  • Emergency preparedness notice — FDNY Two versions: non-combustible fireproof, and combustible non-fireproof. Posting the wrong one for your building type counts as not posting it. Inside every apartment door and in the lobby.
  • "Shut the Door" notice — Local Law 115, FDNY
  • ABCs of Housing notice — Admin Code § 26-1103 Within view of where mail is delivered.
  • Annual lead paint and window guard notice — DOHMH approved form Goes out January 1–15 each year. Our annual notice tracker handles the follow-up.
  • Bedbug annual filing receipt Generated when you file with HPD. Post it or hand it to tenants at lease and renewal, along with the "Stop Bed Bugs Safely" brochure.

Official forms are on HPD's required signage page. Not sure what's missing from your lobby? Walk it with the signage checklist first.
