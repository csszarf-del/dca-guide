New York City

NYC has more posted notices than anywhere else in the country

Fourteen signs in the lobby, an annual mailing every January, and a gas alarm mandate arriving in 2027. Here's what's required, when it's due, and what an inspector writes up when it isn't there.

Deadline · January 1, 2027

Every gas apartment in NYC needs an alarm

Local Law 157 requires a UL 1484 natural gas alarm in every dwelling unit with gas service. Roughly 3.6 million apartments citywide, and close to 90% of NYC homes use gas.

Battery units can be installed by your own staff — no electrician, no permit. Tenant reimbursement for a combined smoke/CO/gas device is capped at $75.
