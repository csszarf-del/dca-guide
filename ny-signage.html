Free checklist · New York City

Every sign your lobby is supposed to have

A housing inspector coming for any reason will look at your signage. Missing notices are the easiest violation to write and the easiest to avoid. Walk the lobby, check what's already posted, and print the list of what isn't.

Checked means it's already posted and legible. Leave unchecked anything missing, faded, or out of date — that's what prints.

Building identity & contacts

HPD registration number sign HMC § 27-2107 Entrance hall. Must also show the building street address and superintendent, janitor or housekeeper info.
Superintendent / managing agent notice HMC § 27-2053 Entrance lobby. Name, address and a working telephone number, in legible lettering.
Street address visible from the sidewalk Clearly displayed at the front entrance.
Inspection visits frame MDL § 329 The 6x9 keyhole frame for the inspection record card.

Life safety notices

Smoke detecting devices notice 28 RCNY § 12-01 Class A multiple dwellings. Posted at or near the mailboxes, on an HPD-approved form.
Carbon monoxide detecting device notice HMC § 27-2046.1 Common area, HPD-approved form.
Gas leak procedures notice Local Law 153 of 2016 All residential multiple dwellings. Must follow HPD's graphical spec.
Emergency preparedness notice Buildings with 3+ apartments. Inside every apartment entrance door AND in the lobby. Two versions exist — one for non-combustible fireproof buildings, one for combustible non-fireproof. Posting the wrong one still counts as missing.
"Shut the Door" fire safety notice Local Law 115 Conspicuous locations. Must be replaced promptly when damaged or removed.
Boiler room key notices — two of them One in the lobby, one on the boiler room entrance door. Name and location of the person holding the keys.
The combined notice trap. Since October 2017 you may post a single combined sign covering gas leak procedures, smoke detectors and CO alarms. Plenty of non-compliant "combined" signs are sold in NYC, and owners have been fined for posting signs that look right but don't meet HPD's specification. If you're using a combined sign, verify it against HPD's published form before trusting it.

Tenant information

Housing Information Guide notice — "ABCs of Housing" Admin Code § 26-1103 Conspicuous spot within view of where mail is delivered.
Garbage collection schedule Required in the lobby where there's no 24-hour dumbwaiter service. Must be behind plastic or in a frame so it can't be tampered with.
Bedbug annual filing receipt Post it prominently, or hand it to every tenant at new lease and renewal. The "Stop Bed Bugs Safely" brochure must also be posted or distributed.
Rent stabilized units notice Only if the building has one or more rent-stabilized units. English and Spanish, common area, must include the building address and HPD property registration number.

Situational — only when it applies

Safe Construction Bill of Rights Posted or distributed when applying for a construction permit that isn't minor repairs, when notifying DOB of emergency work, or when applying for a temporary certificate of occupancy.
Tenant Protection Plan Required by DOB when units stay occupied during construction.
Notices must be large enough to be readily seen in public areas. A sign that's technically present but faded, covered, or hung behind a door is treated the same as a missing one.
